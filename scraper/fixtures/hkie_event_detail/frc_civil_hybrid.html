Event Description:

Technical Seminar: Infrastructure Construction using Fibre Reinforced Concrete

Organised by: HKIE Civil Division and IOM3(HK)
Date/Time: 26 November 2026 , 07:00 - 08:00pm (Registration from 6:30pm)
Venue: Chun Wo & Hsin Chong Room, 10/F, Island Beverley, No 1 Great George Street, Causeway Bay / Hybrid with Webinar (Link to be provided after registration before one day of the event)
Speaker: Mr GAN Cheng Chian (Technical Manager, NV Bekaert SA)
Language: English
Free of Charge

Programme Highlights:
Fibre reinforced concrete (FRC) has been adopted for concrete applications in Singapore and parts of Southeast Asia since the publication of Singapore Standard SS 674: 2021 – Fibre concrete – Design of fibre concrete structures. This presentation will introduce the main properties of FRC and how to measure or calculate them. Some examples of applications of fibre reinforced concrete around Asia will also be introduced. It will conclude with the contribution of FRC in sustainable low-carbon concrete.

Speaker:
Mr. Gan has 37 years of civil engineering experience. Currently working for NV Bekaert SA as Technical Manager, Underground Solutions. 28 years working for Bekaert - involved in many prestigious underground and construction projects around Asia - including Qinling Railway & Highway Tunnel Xian, China; Mandai Underground Cavern Singapore, BMW Shenyang, China, Daimler Chrysler Beijing, China, Busan International Exhibition & Convention Center, South Korea and Jurong Rock Cavern Singapore. Involved in over 22 sprayed fibre concrete tunnel linings and over 20 fibre concrete precast segmental and cast concrete tunnel lining projects since 1998. Member of the Working Group on SS674-2021 Design of fibre concrete structures since Jan 2019. Domain Expert for Sprayed Concrete and Fibre Reinforced Concrete under Intelligent National Productivity and Quality Specification Singapore since Mar 2019.
