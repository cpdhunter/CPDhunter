Online Seminar on “ Office Practitioners: Effective Strategies for Managing Workplace Verbal Abuse” for Sedentary and Professional Services Industry
分享

CPD unit under Hong Kong Safety and Health Certification Scheme: 1.25

In today's changing economic and workplace environment, increasing work and personal pressures can give rise to misunderstandings and conflicts in workplace communication, both internal and client relationships along with the workplace atmosphere.

To support the development of a positive communication culture, OSHC’s Sedentary and Professional Safety & Health Committees is organizing an online seminar in which a Senior Manager & Registered Social Worker from the Richmond Fellowship of Hong Kong will share insights into workplace communication challenges, practical coping strategies and Nonviolent Communication (NVC) techniques to help office practitioners and managers strengthen communication skills, resolve interpersonal conflicts, enhance emotional resilience and foster a harmonious workplace. You are welcome to join.

To enroll, please register with your email address, which should be the same as your Zoom login. A confirmation email with the online seminar link and details will be sent upon successful registration.
