Six Sigma Green Belt Training – Day 2 (of 4)

November 13, 2026
9:30am - 5:00pm
TQM Training Centre, 11/F Lockhart Centre, 301-307 Lockhart Road, Hong Kong

This course is also available on Zoom.

HK$8,000 (HK$8,000 for HKIE Members)

OBJECTIVES

  • Be equipped with problem solving, analytical and business acumen skills
  • Implement successful change management in your company/ organization
  • Identify the root cause of a problem and prevent its recurrence
  • Apply the five-step DMAIC model as a framework to organize process improvement activities
  • Lead a successful process improvement project to deliver meaningful results to your company/ organization

PROGRAMME HIGHLIGHTS

  • 6 Sigma Overview
  • DMAIC Methodology
  • Defining quality improvement projects
  • Introduction to basic statistics
  • Statistical process control
  • Control correlation and regression
  • Analysis of variance
  • New 7 tools
  • Failure modes and effect analysis
  • Poka Yoke and improvement assessment
