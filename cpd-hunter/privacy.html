私隱政策

最後更新：
重點摘要：本網站不設帳戶，不會要求你提供姓名、電郵或任何聯絡資料，亦沒有自設伺服器或資料庫。你的學科設定及 CPD 紀錄只會儲存在你自己裝置的瀏覽器內，我們不會收到，亦無法查看。本網站不使用 cookies、分析工具或廣告追蹤。

「CPD Hunter」（下稱「本網站」）由 （下稱「我們」）營運。我們尊重你的個人資料私隱，並致力遵從香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）的規定。本政策說明本網站如何處理與你有關的資料。

1. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

為提供功能，本網站會透過瀏覽器的本機儲存功能（localStorage），在你使用的裝置上儲存以下資料：

  • 學科設定：你選擇的主要學科及相關界別，用於顯示 DSTM 或 OTM 類別；
  • CPD 紀錄：你自行加入的活動名稱、日期、時數、類別及形式，用於計算你的 CPD 進度；
  • 介面偏好：會員類別及語言。

上述資料不會傳送到我們或任何伺服器，因此我們並沒有收集或持有這些資料。提供這些資料屬自願性質；不提供的話，你仍可瀏覽所有活動。

2. 網站寄存服務

本網站由 GitHub Pages（GitHub, Inc.，Microsoft 旗下公司）寄存。根據 GitHub 的說明，瀏覽 GitHub Pages 網站時，訪客的 IP 地址會被記錄及儲存，作保安用途。這些紀錄由 GitHub 按其 GitHub 私隱聲明處理，可能儲存在香港以外地方，包括美國。我們不會取得或使用這些紀錄來識別個別訪客。

3. Cookies 及追蹤

本網站不會設置 cookies，亦不使用任何分析、廣告或社交媒體追蹤工具。如日後加入，我們會先更新本政策。

4. 第三方網站

當你按連結前往主辦機構或其他網站，例如 HKIE、CIC、OSHC 或網上研討會平台，你在這些網站提供的資料會由該機構按其私隱政策處理，與本網站無關。報名前請細閱有關機構的個人資料收集聲明。

5. 活動資料

本網站只收集公開網頁上的活動資料，例如活動名稱、日期、CPD 時數、主辦機構及連結，不會刻意收集個人資料。如活動資料包含講者姓名等公開資料，有關人士可按第 9 條要求我們移除。

6. 保安

由於資料儲存在你的裝置上，其安全取決於你的裝置及瀏覽器。在公用或共用電腦上使用後，可於「設定」刪除資料。

7. 資料保存及刪除

資料會一直保存在你的瀏覽器，直至你自行刪除。刪除方法如下：

  • 在「設定 → 私隱及資料」按「刪除本機所有資料」；或
  • 清除瀏覽器中本網站的網站資料。

請注意：清除瀏覽器資料、使用無痕模式或轉換裝置，均可能令你的 CPD 紀錄消失，且我們無法替你復原。建議定期使用「匯出 CSV」備份。

8. 查閱及改正資料

你可以隨時直接在本網站查看、修改或刪除上述資料。由於我們並不持有這些資料，我們無法代你查閱或改正。如你認為我們持有你的任何個人資料，可按第 9 條的方法聯絡我們，提出查閱或改正資料要求。

9. 聯絡我們

如對本政策有任何查詢，或要求查閱、改正或移除資料，請電郵至 ，收件人為 。

10. 政策修訂

我們可不時修訂本政策，修訂後的版本會在本頁公布並更新日期。如本網站日後開始收集個人資料，例如會員帳戶或電郵通知服務，我們會在收集資料時或之前另行提供個人資料收集聲明。

本政策備有中文及英文版本。如兩個版本有任何不一致之處，以中文版本為準。

Privacy Policy

Last updated:
In short: the Site has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email or other contact details. It has no server of its own and no database. Your discipline settings and CPD log are stored only in your own browser on your own device. We do not receive them and cannot see them. The Site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

"CPD Hunter" (the "Site") is operated by ("we", "us"). We respect your personal data privacy and are committed to complying with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong. This Policy explains how the Site handles information about you.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the Site stores the following on your device only, using your browser's local storage (localStorage):

  • discipline settings: your main discipline and related disciplines, used to show DSTM or OTM categories;
  • CPD log: activity names, dates, hours, categories and modes you add yourself, used to calculate your CPD progress;
  • interface preferences: membership type and language.

This information is never sent to us or to any server, so we do not collect or hold it. Providing it is voluntary; you can still browse all events without it.

2. Hosting

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft subsidiary). According to GitHub, when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. These logs are handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement and may be stored outside Hong Kong, including in the United States. We do not obtain or use these logs to identify individual visitors.

3. Cookies and Tracking

The Site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media tracking tools. If we add any in the future, we will update this Policy first.

4. Third-Party Websites

When you follow a link to an organiser or another website, such as the HKIE, CIC, OSHC or a webinar platform, any information you give there is handled by that organisation under its own privacy policy, not by the Site. Please read the organiser's personal information collection statement before you register.

5. Event Information

The Site collects only event information from public web pages, such as titles, dates, CPD hours, organisers and links. It does not intentionally collect personal data. If an event listing includes publicly available details such as a speaker's name, that person may ask us to remove it as described in section 9.

6. Security

Because your information is stored on your device, its security depends on your device and browser. After using a public or shared computer, you can delete your data in "Settings".

7. Retention and Deletion

Your information stays in your browser until you delete it. You can delete it by:

  • pressing "Delete all local data" under "Settings → Privacy and Data"; or
  • clearing the Site's data in your browser settings.

Please note that clearing browser data, using private browsing or switching devices may remove your CPD log, and we cannot recover it for you. We recommend backing up regularly with "Export CSV".

8. Access and Correction

You can view, change or delete this information at any time, directly on the Site. As we do not hold it, we cannot access or correct it on your behalf. If you believe we hold any of your personal data, you may contact us as described in section 9 to make a data access or correction request.

9. Contact Us

For any questions about this Policy, or to request access to, correction of or removal of data, please email , addressed to .

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the Site later starts collecting personal data, for example for member accounts or email alerts, we will provide a separate personal information collection statement at or before the time of collection.

This Policy is available in Chinese and English. If there is any inconsistency between the two versions, the Chinese version prevails.
